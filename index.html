Operating Systems for Founder-Led Companies

Some things in your company only work because you are there. That is the problem.

Your team is capable. But certain functions still depend on your judgment, your context, and your involvement at every step. When you step back, things slow down or stop.

We identify which functions depend on you unnecessarily and turn them into systems your team can run independently. The measure of success is simple: how often does that function come back to you before, and how often after.

How it works

We observe, extract, build and hand over.

01

Find the dependency

We look at what your team handles well and what consistently comes back to you. We map the gap between what they know and what you know.

02

Extract the knowledge

We start with actual work, rather than asking you to document everything you know. We take what you have already produced and find the knowledge, context and decision rules your team is missing.

03

Build the system

We build a repeatable workflow around the knowledge, context and decision rules your team is missing. The output holds your quality standard without you reviewing everything.

04

Hand it over

Fixed timeline. Clean exit. Your team owns the system. We document it so they can run it, maintain it and improve it without coming back to us.
